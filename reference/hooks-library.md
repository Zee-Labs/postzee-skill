# Hooks Library — 80+ Proven Templates

Hooks make or break content. The first 1-3 seconds of a video, slide 1 of a carousel, first line of a caption. These templates are battle-tested.

---

## How to use this library

1. Pick the **category** that fits the content type
2. Choose 2-3 templates that fit the topic
3. Customize with specific numbers, names, claims
4. **A/B test** when possible — write 3 hook variations, pick the strongest
5. **Adapt to language and tone** — PT-BR direct vs EN-US punchy vs ES warm

---

## Category 1: Number + Benefit

The most reliable hook category. Specific numbers create credibility.

1. "[N] coisas que todo [persona] precisa saber em [year]"
2. "[N] erros que matam [outcome]"
3. "[N] hacks de [skill] que mudaram minha vida"
4. "[N] ferramentas que substituíram [common alternative]"
5. "[N] frases que todo [persona] usa"
6. "[N] hábitos que separam [winners] de [losers]"
7. "[N] coisas que aprendi em [time period] de [activity]"
8. "[N] sinais de que você [is/isn't doing X]"
9. "[N] verdades duras sobre [topic]"
10. "Os [N] minutos mais importantes do meu dia"

**Examples:**
- "7 erros que matam carrosséis no Instagram"
- "5 verdades duras sobre construir um SaaS sozinho"
- "10 frases que todo founder de sucesso usa"

## Visual hook patterns (for video / slide 1)

Beyond text, the **visual** in the first second matters as much as the words.

### Movement hooks
- **Pattern interrupt:** sudden zoom-in, jump cut, color flash
- **Walking & talking:** subject moving = brain pays attention
- **Hand gesture:** point at camera, hold up object, count fingers

### Composition hooks
- **Direct eye contact:** subject looks straight into lens
- **Close-up extreme:** face fills frame
- **Top-down POV:** rare angle = pattern interrupt

### Text overlay hooks
- **Massive text:** 60-100pt fills 60-70% of slide
- **Single bold claim:** one phrase, max impact
- **Number + curiosity:** "I made $10K..." stops scroll

### Color hooks
- **High contrast:** solid bright background + bold text
- **Brand-recognizable:** consistent palette = instant recognition
- **Unexpected color:** lime green, hot pink, neon yellow against muted feed

## Hook A/B testing framework

When unsure, generate 3 hook variations:

```
HOOK A — Number + Benefit:    "7 erros que matam carrosséis"
HOOK B — Pain + Relief:        "Pare de cometer esses erros em carrosséis"
HOOK C — Curiosity Gap:        "Por que seus carrosséis não bombam"
```

Then pick by:
1. **Specificity** — more concrete = better
2. **Pain level** — does it activate emotion?
3. **Brand fit** — matches your tone?
4. **Predicted save rate** — would you save it?

When in doubt, ask the user: "Tenho 3 hooks possíveis: A) [...] B) [...] C) [...]. Qual ressoa mais com tua marca?"

## Hooks by content goal

### Goal: Save / Bookmark
**Best categories:** Number + Benefit, Mistake / Confession, Step-by-Step
**Why:** users save reference content for later use

**Templates that work:**
- "[N] coisas que você precisa saber sobre [topic]"
- "Os [N] erros que cometi e que você não precisa cometer"
- "Guia completo de [topic] em [N] passos"

### Goal: Comment / Engagement
**Best categories:** Polarizing, Question, Story
**Why:** controversial / personal = forces reaction

**Templates:**
- "Opinião impopular: [statement]"
- "Você concorda que [bold claim]?"
- "Aconteceu comigo: [story setup]"

### Goal: Share / Tag
**Best categories:** Mythbusting, Bold Claim, Pain + Relief
**Why:** shareable when it validates the audience or surprises

**Templates:**
- "[Industry standard] está errado"
- "Marca o [persona] que precisa ver isso"
- "Pare de [common mistake] — compartilha com quem ainda faz"

### Goal: Click / Conversion
**Best categories:** Curiosity Gap, Time-bound, BAB transformation
**Why:** promise + prove + push to next step

**Templates:**
- "[Achievement] em [time]. Aqui está como."
- "O segredo de [topic] que ninguém te conta. Tudo no link."
- "[Before state] → [After state]. Te mostro o método."

## Hook anti-patterns (avoid these)

- ❌ "Hoje vou falar sobre..." (zero hook, pure setup)
- ❌ "Olá pessoal, tudo bem?" (algorithm sees this as filler)
- ❌ "Confiram esse vídeo!" (commands without payoff)
- ❌ "Esse é o post mais importante que você vai ler hoje" (over-promising, generic)
- ❌ "Você sabia que [boring fact]?" (low-energy)
- ❌ Vague: "Algumas dicas pra melhorar seu marketing"
- ❌ Self-aggrandizing: "Como eu virei especialista em..."
- ❌ Long-winded: "Eu queria compartilhar uma coisa que aconteceu comigo essa semana e que..."

## Cultural adaptations

### Portuguese (BR)
- Direct + warm: "Olha só:" / "Vou te contar uma coisa..."
- Brazilian humor: leverages slang carefully (depends on audience)
- Avoid: stiff/formal Portuguese (sounds AI-generated)

### English (US/UK)
- Punchy + direct: "Here's the truth:" / "Stop doing X"
- Self-deprecating ok: "I used to be terrible at..."
- Avoid: overly British formality on TikTok

### Spanish (LatAm)
- Warm + relational: "Mira lo que descubrí..." / "Te voy a contar..."
- Engagement through "tú/ustedes": pick formality based on audience
- Avoid: Castilian Spanish for LatAm audience (cultural mismatch)

### French
- Sophisticated + curious: "Une vérité que personne ne dit:..."
- Tension between formal and casual depending on niche
- Avoid: overly informal slang in B2B

## Building YOUR hook style

After 100+ posts, you'll find a **signature hook style** — specific patterns that resonate with your audience. Track which hooks drive your best engagement and replicate.

**Example signature styles:**
- Alex Hormozi: "If you're [X], you should [Y]."
- Justin Welsh: "[Number] [things]. [Time]. [Outcome]."
- Codie Sanchez: "[Bold investment claim]. Here's why."
- Naval Ravikant: Aphoristic, no hook needed

## Quick hook generator (for the agent)

When user asks for a hook, run this mental checklist:

1. **Identify content type:** listicle / story / mythbusting / launch / BTS?
2. **Pick category** from this library that matches
3. **Generate 3 variations** using different templates
4. **Adapt to tone + language** intelligently
5. **Show user** with reasoning: "Pick A for save-driven, B for engagement, C for shares"

Then refine based on user's choice.
