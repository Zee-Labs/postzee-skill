# Captions Frameworks — Copy That Converts

The caption is **half the post**. A great visual with a generic caption underperforms; an average visual with a phenomenal caption can go viral. This guide gives you proven frameworks per platform.

> **Examples below are written in English as canonical templates.** Always render the final caption in the user's language, adapting tone and idioms naturally. The framework structure (AIDA, PAS, BAB, FAB, 4Ps) is universal.

---

## The 5 copywriting frameworks (memorize these)

### AIDA — Attention → Interest → Desire → Action

**Best for:** Sales, conversion-driven posts, ads.

```
[ATTENTION — bold hook stopping the scroll]
[INTEREST — develop the problem/opportunity in 2-3 lines]
[DESIRE — paint the result/transformation]
[ACTION — clear CTA]
```

**Example (Postzee feature launch):**

> Still losing 4 hours a week scheduling posts manually? ⚡
>
> Most solo founders spend more time managing the calendar than creating content.
>
> Imagine scheduling 30 days of posts in 15 minutes — on every network at once.
>
> Start free: link in bio 👇

---

### PAS — Problem → Agitation → Solution

**Best for:** Educational content, problem-aware audiences.

```
[PROBLEM — name the pain point in 1 line]
[AGITATION — twist the knife with consequences]
[SOLUTION — your answer or framework]
```

**Example:**

> You're posting every day and not growing.
>
> It's not the algorithm. You're missing a hook in the first 3 seconds. 90% of videos that fail, fail on slide 1.
>
> Use these 5 hooks that always work: [...]

---

### BAB — Before → After → Bridge

**Best for:** Selling transformation, course creators, coaches.

```
[BEFORE — the painful current state]
[AFTER — the desired future state]
[BRIDGE — how to get there (your offer/method)]
```

**Example:**

> Before: 200 followers, 3 likes per post, ready to quit.
>
> After: 47K followers, weekly viral posts, 2 brand offers per week.
>
> What changed? I stopped chasing trends. I started applying ONE strategy: [...]

---

### FAB — Features → Advantages → Benefits

**Best for:** Product launches, feature announcements.

```
[FEATURES — what it is/does (objective)]
[ADVANTAGES — why it's better]
[BENEFITS — what user achieves (emotional/concrete)]
```

**Example:**

> Postzee now generates full videos with AI — 30s cinematic video with captions in any language.
>
> Unlike other tools, we connect models like Sora 2 and Veo 3.1 with automatic scheduling across 30+ networks.
>
> Create 1 month of video content in one afternoon. No video editor. No production house.

---

### 4 Ps — Picture → Promise → Prove → Push

**Best for:** Long-form storytelling, LinkedIn posts.

```
[PICTURE — paint vivid scene of dream outcome]
[PROMISE — what you're going to deliver]
[PROVE — credibility/data/result]
[PUSH — CTA]
```

**Example:**

> Imagine waking up every day with 3 qualified leads in your inbox — without having to prospect.
>
> I'll show you the exact system I've used for 18 months to attract leads in my sleep.
>
> Result: 47 recurring clients, $30K MRR, no ads. Just strategic organic content.
>
> Save this post — I'll post the step-by-step tomorrow.

## Per-platform templates

### Instagram (Feed + Carousel + Reels)

**Caption length:** 125 chars visible before "see more". The first 125 chars MUST hook.

**Sweet spot:** 150-300 chars total for engagement, longer (500-2200) for storytelling.

**Hashtags:** 3-5 max in 2026 (down from 30). Use niche hashtags.

**Template (BAB for Reels):**

```
[Line 1 — bold hook]
[Empty line]
[Line 2-3 — Before state]
[Empty line]
[Line 4-5 — After state]
[Empty line]
[Line 6 — Bridge / what they need to do]
[Empty line]
[CTA]
.
.
.
#niche1 #niche2 #niche3 #niche4 #niche5
```

**Example caption for the 7-tools carousel:**

```
I tested 47 tools in 2025.

These 7 are the ones that survived my daily routine as a solo founder.

From task management to marketing automation, all optimized for ROI.

→ Save this carousel to build your stack
→ Comment your favorite tool 👇

#solopreneur #productivity #digitaltools #founder #saas
```

### LinkedIn

**Caption length:** First 3 lines visible (~210 chars). HOOK MUST FIT.

**Sweet spot:** 1300-2000 chars total for high engagement.

**Hashtags:** 3-5 niche hashtags at the end.

**Template (4 Ps for LinkedIn):**

```
[Line 1 — Picture: vivid scene]
[Line 2 — continuation, dramatic pause]
[Line 3 — promise / curiosity gap]

[Body — Prove with data + story]
[Numbered list or formatted bullets]

[Closing thought / philosophy]

[Push: question or CTA]

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3
```

**Example:**

```
3 years ago I was in severe burnout.
Working 80h/week on a SaaS that wasn't taking off.
Today, same team, I work 30h/week and we earn 5x more.

The difference? I stopped doing 7 things:

1. Internal meetings without a written agenda
2. Replying to email outside a defined window
3. Accepting client features without analysis
4. Working weekends as "extra effort"
5. Skipping lunch to "save time"
6. Multitasking during deep work
7. Believing that effort = results

Productivity isn't doing more. It's doing less, better.

Which of these do you still do? 👇

#productivity #leadership #saas
```

### TikTok / Reels (vertical video)

**Caption length:** Short. ~80-150 chars.

**Hashtags:** 3-5 niche + 1 trending.

**Template:**

```
[Hook line — same energy as the video]

[Optional: 1 supporting thought]

[CTA: comment, save, follow]

#niche1 #niche2 #niche3
```

**Example:**

```
the mistake that keeps 90% of videos from going viral 🚨

save this to review before your next post 📌

#digitalmarketing #contentcreator #tiktoktips
```

### X / Twitter

**Caption length:** 280 chars max per tweet. Threads for longer.

**No hashtags** (signal of low quality on X in 2026).

**Template (single tweet):**

```
[1 hook line — sharp, declarative]
[Empty line]
[1-2 lines support]
[Empty line]
[CTA implied or explicit]
```

**Example:**

```
Most carousels fail because of slide 1.

If your hook isn't massive, bold, and brain-stopping in 1 second, the other 9 slides don't matter.

Fix slide 1 first.
```

**Thread template:**

```
TWEET 1 (hook): "[promise + curiosity]"
TWEET 2-N (each one valuable standalone): "...point...example..."
TWEET N+1 (recap): "TL;DR — [synthesis]"
TWEET N+2 (CTA): "Follow @user for more like this. RT the first tweet if you found this useful."
```

### YouTube (description)

**First 100 chars** show in search/feed snippets — write a hook.

**Sweet spot:** 200-500 chars for first paragraph; full description can be 1000+.

**Template:**

```
[Hook — 1-2 lines of why to watch]

[Mini timeline of chapters — boosts retention]
0:00 Intro
1:23 [chapter]
4:56 [chapter]

[Resources mentioned + links]

[Subscribe CTA + community links]
```

### Pinterest

**Caption length:** 100-500 chars.

**Keywords matter more than hashtags** — Pinterest is a visual SEARCH engine.

**Template:**

```
[Keyword-rich title]

[1-2 sentences of context with keywords naturally placed]

[CTA: "Save for later" / "Click for full guide"]
```

### Facebook

**Caption length:** First 80 chars visible. Up to 500 chars for engagement.

**Template (similar to Instagram but slightly longer-form):**

```
[Hook — 1-2 lines]
[Body — 3-5 lines of value]
[Question or CTA]
```

## Hook formulas (proven)

These can plug into any framework. See `hooks-library.md` for 50+ templates.

### Top 10 hook formulas

1. **Number + benefit:** "7 things every X needs to know"
2. **Pain + relief:** "Stop doing X. Do Y instead."
3. **Bold claim:** "This is the only [framework] you need."
4. **Time-bound:** "What I learned in 12 months of [topic]"
5. **Curiosity gap:** "I made $X in 30 days using this..."
6. **Polarizing:** "Unpopular opinion: [thing] is overrated"
7. **Question:** "Why do 90% of [people] fail at [thing]?"
8. **Story setup:** "3 years ago, I was [bad state]. Today, [good state]."
9. **Mistake reveal:** "The biggest mistake I made in [topic]"
10. **Behind the scenes:** "How I [did something impressive] (real BTS)"

## CTA library

### High-engagement CTAs by intent

> Templates in English — translate to the user's language and adapt the idiom.

**For SAVES (algorithmic boost):**
- "Save this for later 📌"
- "Bookmark this for later"
- "Pin this so you don't lose it"

**For COMMENTS (algorithm + social proof):**
- "Which of these do you use? 👇"
- "Comment your biggest pain point with [topic]"
- "Agree? Disagree? Tell me in the comments"
- "Comment 'KEYWORD' and I'll send the template"

**For SHARES (organic reach):**
- "Tag someone who needs to see this"
- "Share with that friend who..."
- "Tag a founder who needs this"

**For DM (lead gen):**
- "DM 'GUIDE' and I'll send the full material"
- "Comment 'READY' and I'll DM you the checklist"

**For LINK CLICK:**
- "Link in bio for the full guide"
- "Click the bio link if you want to start today"
- "Everything broken down in the article (link in bio)"

**For FOLLOW:**
- "Follow @user for more [topic]"
- "Follow for daily [niche] tips"

**Don't use:** "What do you think?" (vague, no engagement)

## Hashtag strategy 2026

**Reality check:** hashtags ≠ what they used to be. Social SEO now matters more.

### Per platform

| Platform | Hashtag count | Strategy |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| Instagram | 3-5 | Niche > generic. Mix 2 broad + 3 niche |
| LinkedIn | 3-5 | Industry + topic + community |
| TikTok | 3-5 | 1 trending + 3-4 niche |
| X | 0-2 | Skip them (penalizes) |
| Pinterest | 0 | Use keywords in title/description |
| YouTube | 3-5 in description | Plus keyword-rich title |
| Facebook | 1-3 | Optional, low impact |

### Niche vs broad

- ❌ `#marketing` (1B+ posts, instantly buried)
- ✅ `#marketingsaaspme` (specific community, you stand out)

### Banned/shadowbanned hashtags
Periodically check — some hashtags are flagged. Avoid `#follow4follow`, `#like4like`, generic spam-flagged tags.

### Brand hashtag

If you have a brand/community: 1 branded hashtag in every post (`#postzeefamily`).

## Caption + carousel combo (highest performing)

The carousel and the caption are two hooks. The caption hooks people who *might not* swipe.

**Carousel slide 1:** Visual hook (image + bold text)
**Caption first 125 chars:** Different hook angle

**Example:**

Carousel slide 1: **"7 tools every solo founder needs in 2026"**

Caption hook: *"I tested 47 tools in 2025. These 7 are the ones that survived. ⤵️"*

→ **Two angles, two chances to stop the scroll.**

Don't repeat the slide 1 text in the caption. Use a *complementary* hook.

## Tone calibration (intelligent inference)

Match tone to:
- **The brand** (if user has a known voice)
- **The audience** (founders ≠ teenagers ≠ enterprise B2B)
- **The platform** (LinkedIn formal-ish, TikTok casual-energetic)
- **The content type** (story = warm, listicle = punchy, BTS = authentic)

### Tone palette

| Tone | When | Example phrases |
|------|------|-----------------|
| **Educational-friendly** | How-tos, tips | "Here's a quick insight for you..." |
| **Punchy-confident** | Bold takes, hooks | "I'll be direct:" / "Fact:" |
| **Warm-storytelling** | Personal stories | "Let me tell you something..." |
| **Provocative-polarizing** | Mythbusting | "Unpopular opinion:" / "You may disagree, but..." |
| **Hype-energetic** | Launches, sales | "JUST LAUNCHED!" / "You need to see this" |
| **Calm-authoritative** | Thought leadership | "After 10 years of [topic], I learned that..." |
| **Inclusive-conversational** | Community building | "I'm curious: what would you do?" |

When undecided, **infer from these signals:**
- User's prior posts (if visible)
- Industry (B2B SaaS = professional / Lifestyle = warm)
- Topic gravity (serious issue = calm-authoritative; light topic = punchy-confident)
- Platform (LinkedIn ≠ TikTok)

**Default:** "punchy-confident with warmth" — works for 80% of cases.

## Multilingual captions

If user wants the same content in multiple languages:
- **Don't auto-translate literally** — adapt cultural references
- **Hashtags differ by language** — e.g. `#produtividade` (PT) vs `#productivity` (EN) vs `#productividad` (ES)
- **CTAs adapt** — e.g. "Save for later" (EN) ↔ "Salva pra depois" (PT) ↔ "Guárdalo para después" (ES)
- **Tone may shift** — PT-BR is warmer than EN-US which is more direct; FR can be more formal in B2B; DE values precision

When multilingual, generate per-language caption, not single translation.

## Caption checklist before posting

- [ ] Hook in first 125 chars (Instagram visible) or first 3 lines (LinkedIn)
- [ ] Framework applied (AIDA / PAS / BAB / FAB / 4Ps)
- [ ] Tone matches brand + audience + platform
- [ ] CTA is specific and action-oriented (not "what do you think")
- [ ] Hashtag count platform-appropriate (3-5 typical)
- [ ] Hashtags niche, not generic
- [ ] Line breaks for readability (no walls of text)
- [ ] Emojis purposeful, not decorative (or none if brand demands)
- [ ] No typos / grammar errors (proofread!)
- [ ] First word capital, last word punchy
- [ ] No banned/shadowbanned hashtags

---

## Common caption mistakes

- ❌ Burying the hook (paragraph of throat-clearing before the point)
- ❌ Generic CTA ("what do you think?")
- ❌ 30 hashtags (signals spam in 2026)
- ❌ Translating literally across languages
- ❌ Walls of text on Instagram (need line breaks)
- ❌ Repeating slide 1 text in carousel caption
- ❌ Inconsistent tone (hype hook + corporate body)
- ❌ Forgetting the @ for collabs/credits
- ❌ Over-promising in hook, under-delivering in body
- ❌ Same caption template every post (looks AI-generated)
