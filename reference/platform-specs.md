## Universal best practices

### Frame rate
- Always **30fps** for social (60fps reserved for sports/gaming)
- AI-generated videos often output 24-25fps → re-encode to 30 if needed

### Audio loudness
- **-14 LUFS** is the modern standard across IG/TikTok/YouTube
- Use `loudnorm` ffmpeg filter (see `ffmpeg-cookbook.md` Section 4)

### Codec
- Video: H.264 (compatibility) or H.265 (smaller file, but check platform)
- Audio: AAC, 192 kbps minimum

### Container
- MP4 with `+faststart` flag for instant playback in feed

### Color space
- BT.709 for HD content
- Avoid HDR for social — most feed players don't render correctly

### Safe zones (vertical 9:16 — 1080×1920)
- **Top 14%** (top 270px): covered by username/profile UI
- **Bottom 18%** (bottom 350px): covered by caption/buttons
- **Sides 5%** (left/right 50px): may be cropped on some devices

Keep important text/visuals in the center 60% (top 270px to bottom 1570px, sides 50px-1030px).

### Brand-safe content
- Profanity filters vary by platform (TikTok strict, X loose)
- Avoid copyrighted music in originals (use platform native libraries)
- Health/political claims trigger reduced reach algorithms

## Cross-posting checklist

When user wants to post same content across platforms:

1. **Re-aspect:** generate or convert to platform-correct aspect
2. **Re-trim:** Reels max 90s, Stories 15s, TikTok 10min
3. **Re-caption:** different lengths and tones per platform
4. **Re-hashtag:** different counts and language
5. **Re-CTA:** "swipe up" works in some, not others; "link in bio" universal
6. **Re-music:** use platform-native music library (avoids copyright issues)

Never just "blast same content everywhere" — always adapt.
