# Carousel References — Two Worked Examples

This file shows the methodology applied end-to-end on two real topics. Use it to calibrate intuition: what good looks like at every stage — triagem, headline batch, chosen headline, 18-block spine, CTA bridge, caption.

Each example is a **full editorial walk-through**. Copy the *discipline*, not the topics — when generating, draft fresh content for the user's actual brief.

## Example 1 — Tese Contraintuitiva

**Brief from the user:**
- Handle: `@criadora.online`
- Niche: creator economy / educational content
- Hex: `#7C3AED` (primary) / `#06B6D4` (accent)
- Style: Moderno
- Type: Tese Contraintuitiva
- CTA: "Comenta NICHO pra receber um diagnóstico"
- 9 slides, 0 with images (typography-only)

### Triagem (4-layer analysis)

**Layer 1 — Transformação:** the reader stops believing they should "go deep on what they know best", starts believing the niche-too-deep problem is real and viralization rewards a cross-layer voice.

**Layer 2 — Fricção central:** creators with deep expertise see flat reach; meanwhile generalist creators in adjacent niches grow 10x faster. The fault isn't algorithm — it's the assumption that depth = reach.

**Layer 3 — Ângulo narrativo:** the topic you master the most is the topic you over-explain. Over-explanation reads as effortful, not effortless. The audience rewards effortlessness.

**Layer 4 — Evidências:**
1. Datafolha 2025 — creators in "second-favorite topic" report 3.2x higher engagement
2. Specific case: a brazilian creator who pivoted from law to "law-adjacent culture commentary" — went from 8k to 180k in 11 months
3. Mechanism: pleasure in the topic > expertise in the topic, on social platforms

## Example 2 — Tendência Interpretada

**Brief from the user:**
- Handle: `@runlab.br`
- Niche: corrida / saúde mental
- Hex: `#1A1A2E` (primary) / `#FF6B35` (accent)
- Style: Bold
- Type: Tendência Interpretada
- CTA: "Comenta CORRIDA pra receber a planilha"
- 9 slides, 3 com imagem (cover + slides 4 e 6 full-bleed)

### Triagem (4-layer analysis)

**Layer 1 — Transformação:** the reader stops seeing corrida as a "fitness trend" and starts seeing it as the symptom of a generational shift in how anxious adults regulate themselves. The carousel is not motivational — it's diagnostic.

**Layer 2 — Fricção central:** corrida cresceu 187% em São Paulo entre 2019 e 2025. A leitura padrão é "moda". A leitura honesta é: 35M+ adultos brasileiros estão usando corrida como substituta de ansiolíticos / álcool / scroll noturno.

**Layer 3 — Ângulo narrativo:** corrida virou a droga favorita dos adultos ansiosos — e ninguém está olhando pra isso como problema de saúde pública.

**Layer 4 — Evidências:**
1. Datafolha + Federação Paulista de Atletismo: +187% participantes em corridas urbanas SP 2019→2025
2. Caso: clubes de corrida triplicaram no Itaim entre 2022 e 2025
3. Mecanismo: o que a corrida regula neuroquimicamente é exatamente o mesmo eixo que ansiolíticos e álcool regulam

## How to read these examples

The two carousels above are **complete editorial walk-throughs** — not templates to copy. Use them to calibrate:

- **Triagem depth** — see how Layer 1-4 each contributes a distinct angle. Skipping triagem produces flat carousels.
- **Headline batch coverage** — notice how the 10 variations cover different patterns (Death, Investigation, Generational, Two-Colon, Brand-reveal) and triggers (Indignação, Curiosidade, Identidade, Aspiração).
- **Block density** — see how each block hits its word count target. Blocks under-density = thin slides. Over = crowded slides.
- **The frase-ponte** — both examples have one. Without it, the CTA feels like a discontinuity.
- **The honest counterpoint** (slide 7) — both examples include the "yes, but…" that the audience would raise. This is the editorial difference between propaganda and analysis.

Copy the **discipline**, not the topics.

## When to consult this file

- **First time generating a carousel for a user** — read both examples to internalize the bar.
- **When the user's brief feels generic** — re-read the triagem of each example to see how a vague brief gets sharpened into a specific angle.
- **When a slide block feels thin** — find the equivalent block in these examples and compare densities.
- **When the user asks "como assim?" after a headline** — pull a worked variation from the example to show what each pattern produces.

These two examples cover the two arcs the agent uses most often. The Case/Benchmark and Previsão/Futuro arcs follow the same discipline applied to different content — extrapolate from these two.
